Uncomplicated Dengue (Without Warning Signs)

CPG 2026 Fluid Quota Calculator (Critical Phase Without Shock - Group B2)

Fluid quota = 1-day maintenance (4-2-1 / Holliday-Segar) + 5% deficit (50 ml/kg), given over 48 hours, weight capped at 70 kg (CPG 9.3, Appendix 11)

Weight Cap: 70 kg
Important Guideline Rule: Weight used for fluid calculation is strictly capped at 70 kg to prevent fluid overload from the Revised Starling leak. In obesity (BMI >27.5), Adjusted Body Weight (ABW) is applied. The quota is for the critical phase without shock only, and not every patient needs the full volume. DKA/HHS may need more.

Algorithm 1 Infusion Rates

If oral fluids are not tolerated: IV crystalloid 3-5 ml/kg/hr for 1-2 h. If improving, reduce to 2-3 ml/kg/hr for 1-2 h, then 1-2 ml/kg/hr or maintenance, and stop once oral intake is adequate. If not improving and not in shock, take FBC/VBG/lactate and go to 5-7 ml/kg/hr while awaiting results. FBC every 4-12 h. Keep total (oral + IV) within the quota.

Pathophysiology: Revised Starling Law & EGL

Traditional models assumed 90% venular reabsorption. Modern physiology proves fluid leaves solely via lymphatics due to the Endothelial Glycocalyx Layer (EGL). Dengue NS1 activates heparanase and sialidases, breaking the EGL. Intravenous fluids given excessively leak directly into third spaces and will cause severe pulmonary edema during recovery phase reabsorption.

Occult Bleeding vs Plasma Leakage

Plasma leakage manifests as haemoconcentration (rising HCT ≥20%). If a patient is in shock but HCT is unexpectedly normal or dropping, massive occult haemorrhage (retroperitoneal or GI) is occurring. Crystalloids alone will worsen hypoperfusion; emergency cross-match and packed red cells are needed.

Strictly Prohibited Medications

NSAIDs (mefenamic acid, ibuprofen) and aspirin cause gastric mucosal ulceration and irreversible platelet dysfunction in patients with preexisting thrombocytopenia, precipitating catastrophic GI bleeding. Corticosteroids confer no clinical benefit and elevate infection risks.

Special Populations: Why Low Admission Threshold?

In pregnancy, physiological haemodilution masks rising HCT, while resting tachycardia masks early shock. In the elderly, dengue presents atypically with lethargy and leucopenia rather than classic fever. Both groups require a very low threshold for hospital admission.

Outpatient Dengue Home Care & Warning Leaflet

Home Care Advice (English)

For patients suitable for outpatient management

WHAT TO DO:

  • Drink 200 ml fluid every hour while awake (ORS, fruit juice, coconut water, soup).
  • Ensure you pass urine at least 4 to 6 times a day.
  • Take Paracetamol for fever or pain, but never exceed 4,000 mg (8 tablets of 500mg) per day.
  • Attend daily blood tests and doctor appointments without fail.

WHAT TO AVOID:

  • DO NOT take NSAIDs or pain relievers (Ibuprofen, Diclofenac/Voltaren, Ponstan, Aspirin). If you already take these regularly, ask your doctor first.
  • DO NOT take steroids or unverified herbal extracts.
  • DO NOT take intramuscular injections, or have massage, cupping (bekam) or gua sha.

DANGER SIGNS - GO TO HOSPITAL IMMEDIATELY:

General condition or symptoms getting worse as the fever subsides, severe stomach pain, persistent vomiting, extreme tiredness, difficulty or fast breathing, fainting, bleeding from gums/nose or blood in vomit, urine or stools, passing urine less than 4 times a day.

Nasihat Penjagaan Di Rumah (BM)

Untuk pesakit yang dirawat sebagai pesakit luar

APA YANG PERLU DILAKUKAN:

  • Minum air secukupnya 200 ml setiap jam sewaktu jaga (garam hidrasi ORS, jus buah, air kelapa, sup).
  • Pastikan anda membuang air kencing sekurang-kurangnya 4 hingga 6 kali sehari.
  • Ambil Paracetamol jika demam, tidak melebihi 4,000 mg (8 biji 500mg) sehari.
  • Hadir temujanji pemeriksaan darah dan doktor setiap hari.

APA YANG MESTI DIELAKKAN:

  • JANGAN ambil ubat tahan sakit NSAID seperti Ibuprofen, Voltaren, Ponstan, atau Aspirin.
  • JANGAN ambil steroid, ubat tradisional, atau jus herba yang tidak disahkan.
  • JANGAN ambil suntikan ke dalam otot, berbekam, atau mengurut.

TANDA BAHAYA - SEGERA KE KECEMASAN HOSPITAL:

Keadaan badan semakin lemah atau simptom semakin teruk apabila demam kebah, sakit perut yang teruk, muntah berterusan, keletihan melampau, susah bernafas atau pernafasan laju, pitam, pendarahan gusi/hidung atau darah dalam muntah, air kencing atau najis, kencing kurang dari 4 kali sehari.
